# Hồ sơ sản phẩm mục tiêu cho thiết bị siêu âm sản khoa - WHO 2025

## Mục đích và phạm vi sử dụng

### Thiết bị phải hỗ trợ tối thiểu một siêu âm trước 24 tuần
WHO nhắc lại khuyến nghị để mọi thai phụ có khả năng tiếp cận ít nhất **một siêu âm trước 24 tuần**. Thiết bị dùng trong ANC phải hỗ trợ xác định vị trí thai, hoạt động tim, kích thước thai và **GA (Gestational Age - tuổi thai)**, số lượng thai, chorionicity (số bánh nhau) và amnionicity (số buồng ối) ở đa thai. Khi hệ thống và kỹ năng cho phép, sau khoảng 18 tuần có thể đánh giá cấu trúc đầu-mặt-cổ-cột sống-lồng ngực-tim-thành bụng-chi, hình thái/vị trí nhau và dây rốn. Siêu âm không được dùng thay thế toàn bộ chăm sóc trước sinh.

## Làm phẳng bảng TPP: đầu dò, hình ảnh và hiển thị

### Yêu cầu tối thiểu về đầu dò và chế độ hình ảnh
- **Đầu dò bụng:** transabdominal transducer **3-5 MHz** là tối thiểu; ít nhất một cổng đầu dò, trừ thiết bị kết nối không dây với smartphone.
- **Vật tư:** gel, vật liệu/khăn khử khuẩn và găng; nếu dùng đầu dò âm đạo cần bao đầu dò vô khuẩn và tiêu chuẩn phòng nhiễm khuẩn phù hợp.
- **Chế độ:** **2D**, **B-mode** và **M-mode**; 3D/4D **không bắt buộc** cho siêu âm sản khoa cơ bản thường quy.
- **Đo lường/zoom:** đo diện tích, khoảng cách, thể tích, góc, tốc độ/gia tốc; zoom ảnh tĩnh **≥10X**, zoom thời gian thực **≥4X**.
- **Đầu ra:** grayscale thời gian thực, freeze-frame, caliper điện tử, dải động phù hợp; dải tần thường **2-9 MHz**; màn hình số/LCD đủ đọc kết quả và mẫu báo cáo linh hoạt.

### Lưu trữ và truyền dữ liệu
Yêu cầu tối thiểu gồm tự động ghi ảnh freeze-frame; lưu trên thiết bị hoặc tải ra bộ nhớ ngoài; dung lượng tối thiểu **256 GB**; khả năng truyền ảnh qua Bluetooth/wireless. Mục tiêu tối ưu là ghi cả ảnh/video, tải qua Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, dung lượng **1-5 TB** và có phương án cung cấp ảnh khi cần cho mục đích tài liệu. WHO nhấn mạnh lưu trữ dữ liệu phải an toàn; việc cung cấp ảnh không được biến thành **recreational/entertainment ultrasound (siêu âm giải trí)**.

## Tính cơ động, độ bền và đào tạo

### Thiết kế cho nhiều bối cảnh, kể cả nguồn lực hạn chế
Thiết bị dạng xe đẩy phải được **một người** di chuyển và có bánh khóa; thiết bị cầm tay/di động phải dễ đưa lên-xuống phương tiện và mang bằng tay. Thị trường hiện có thiết bị di động khoảng **0,9-13 kg**. Thiết bị phải chịu được sử dụng thường xuyên và lưu trữ/vận hành/làm sạch trong môi trường nóng, lạnh, ẩm, bụi; giao diện thân thiện và có cảnh báo nguồn điện thấp. Hướng dẫn nhanh và user manual phải có ngôn ngữ phù hợp địa phương; cần đào tạo “knobology” theo thiết bị vì chất lượng siêu âm phụ thuộc rất nhiều vào người vận hành.

### Bảo trì, nguồn điện và pin
Yêu cầu TPP bao gồm kế hoạch bảo trì dễ thực hiện, kiểm soát nhiễm khuẩn cho bề mặt/đầu dò và khả năng vận hành bền vững. Nguồn điện cần adapter AC có bảo vệ phù hợp; thiết bị di động cần pin sạc tích hợp. Mục tiêu tối ưu cho thiết bị portable là pin tối thiểu **8 giờ**, có chức năng tiết kiệm pin và nhiều phương thức sạc như điện lưới, ắc quy xe hoặc năng lượng mặt trời; cần tính đến thay pin trong vòng đời thiết bị. Chi phí mua sắm phải xem cả thiết bị, đầu dò, vật tư tiêu hao, vận chuyển, nhập khẩu, bảo trì và tổng chi phí vòng đời.

## An toàn và quản lý chất lượng

### ALARA và sử dụng Doppler có chỉ định
Phơi nhiễm thai với siêu âm phải theo **ALARA (As Low As Reasonably Achievable - thấp nhất có thể đạt được một cách hợp lý)**: thời gian ngắn nhất và công suất thấp nhất đủ để lấy thông tin chẩn đoán. TPP nêu **B-mode và M-mode** có acoustic output thấp và được coi là an toàn ở mọi giai đoạn thai kỳ. Doppler không phải yêu cầu thường quy trong ANC và có mức năng lượng cao hơn; nếu có chỉ định và sử dụng, thermal index hiển thị nên **≤1,0** và thời gian phơi nhiễm ngắn nhất có thể.

### Quy định và bảo hành
Thiết bị phải đáp ứng yêu cầu quản lý quốc gia/khu vực và tiêu chuẩn quản lý chất lượng quốc tế, gồm **ISO 13485:2016**. Yêu cầu tối thiểu về bảo hành: **thiết bị 2-5 năm**, **đầu dò 12 tháng**; mục tiêu tối ưu là thiết bị **≥5 năm** và đầu dò >12 tháng. Bảo hành, khả năng sửa chữa, phụ tùng, pin và bảo trì phải được tính từ đầu để tránh thiết bị ngừng hoạt động sau khi dự án mua sắm kết thúc.

Globally, about 287 000 women die annually from complications related to pregnancy and childbirth, the majority of which occur in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs) (1). In addition, there are nearly 2 million stillbirths and 2.4 million neonatal deaths per year worldwide (2,3). Quality antenatal care provided by skilled health personnel is essential for preventing avoidable mortality and morbidity of women and their babies. Through antenatal care, women are supported with health education, screening, identification of risk factors, preventive strategies, and management or treatment of complications. Despite the importance of access to antenatal care services for all pregnant women, only 66% of pregnant women worldwide receive the recommended four antenatal care visits (4).

### Nguồn gốc: 1.2 Obstetric ultrasound - đoạn 0002

Ultrasound is an important component of routine obstetric care as both a screening and diagnostic tool. The World Health Organization (WHO) 2016 Recommendations for antenatal care for a positive pregnancy experience recommend that pregnant women1 receive at least one antenatal ultrasound scan before 24 weeks of gestation. This provides an accurate assessment of gestational age (GA), improves detection of fetal structural or growth anomalies, and identifies any placental abnormalities that can significantly contribute to maternal–fetal complications (e.g. placenta previa, which can be detected on ultrasound, and increases the risk of antepartum and postpartum haemorrhage) (5). In 2022, WHO updated its recommendation on antenatal ultrasound, reiterating that one imaging ultrasound before 24 weeks of gestation should be available to all pregnant women (6).

### Nguồn gốc: 1.2 Obstetric ultrasound - đoạn 0003

WHO advises that antenatal ultrasound conducted before 24 weeks of gestation should be able to detect the location of the pregnancy, cardiac activity, fetal size and GA, as well as fetal number, chorionicity and amnionicity for multiple gestations. Where the health system and staff skills allow, the presence of a normal head, face, neck, spine, chest, heart, abdominal wall and extremities, placental appearance and location and umbilical cord can also be determined. Assessment of GA is vital to ensure that clinical interventions are used at the right time; for example, administering antenatal corticosteroids before 34 weeks of gestation.

### Nguồn gốc: 1.2 Obstetric ultrasound - đoạn 0004

In addition to WHO recommendations on routine antenatal care, in women with comorbidities (e.g. pre-eclampsia or gestational diabetes) regular ultrasound can also help in monitoring fetal growth and well-being, and thus assist in optimizing the timing of birth. If available, ultrasound can also be used intrapartum to confirm non-cephalic fetal presentation or malposition, or detect the presence of multiple fetuses (7). In the postpartum period, ultrasound can be used for assessing retained products of conception, an important cause of postpartum haemorrhage (8).

While this document refers to women, it is also intended to be applicable to adolescent girls, and trans and gender-diverse people who become pregnant.

### Nguồn gốc: 1.3 Barriers to access to obstetric ultrasound - đoạn 0005

Limited data are available on global access to and coverage of obstetric ultrasound, though available studies suggest that many pregnant women do not have access to it when they need it (9,10). Many countries lack a sufficient number of trained, skilled providers who can use ultrasound devices correctly (11).

### Nguồn gốc: 1.3 Barriers to access to obstetric ultrasound - đoạn 0006

Several barriers restrict implementation of universal access to ultrasound for pregnant women (6). High procurement costs for devices (including consumables, accessories, maintenance and training), lack of health worker capacity to use ultrasound equipment appropriately, limited local maintenance options, and infrastructure requirements, including a safe and secure place for examinations to take place, complicate the scaling up of ultrasound in maternity care settings (6,12). The cost to women and their families, and/or the need to travel long distances to have access to ultrasound, are also barriers (13). Other considerations that must be mitigated include potential misuse of ultrasound, either for financial gain or convenience, concerns about inappropriate use for fetal sex determination, and ultrasound being used in place of routine antenatal care (6,12,14).

### Nguồn gốc: 1.4 Commercially available ultrasound devices - đoạn 0007

Marketed ultrasound devices range from conventional, cart-based models to a variety of portable options (including laptop-based, handheld and tablet/smartphone-connected devices). The market for ultrasound devices is expanding, particularly as technological advances allow for smaller and more portable devices to be developed (14–16). The diagnostic ultrasound market2 is expected to reach US$ 9.3 billion by 2027 (17). A recent systematic review identified at least 102 portable devices available for obstetric ultrasound (18). Limited guidance currently exists on what characteristics (or requirements) ultrasound systems or devices must possess to meet the WHO recommendations on antenatal ultrasound for pregnant women (5).

### Nguồn gốc: 1.4 Commercially available ultrasound devices - đoạn 0008

However, WHO has suggested that ultrasound devices used by appropriately skilled health personnel in antenatal care settings should, at a minimum, be able to assess fetal cardiac activity, fetal number, chorionicity (for multiple gestations), GA and fetal size (6). After 18 weeks gestation, they should be able to detect the presence of a normal head, face, neck, spine, chest, heart, abdomen, abdominal wall and extremities, in addition to placental appearance, location and assessment of the umbilical cord (6).

### Nguồn gốc: 1.5 Purpose of this target product profile - đoạn 0009

Target product profiles (TPPs) are strategic documents that specify the minimum and optimal requirements for new health products, including devices. TPPs guide product developers on the characteristics required of new devices to meet pre-specified clinical and public health needs and can help guide decisions on procurement. They also inform research and development strategies, help frame product dossiers, streamline communication with regulatory agencies and help funders to set targets (19).

For all conditions, not obstetric-specific.

### Nguồn gốc: 1.5 Purpose of this target product profile - đoạn 0010

TPPs have been created by WHO, the United Nations Children’s Fund (UNICEF) and other international organizations to address major public health gaps, including the need to spur development and accelerate implementation of vaccines, antibiotics, HIV treatments and diagnostics for sexually transmitted infection (20–24). WHO has identified obstetric ultrasound devices as a priority area for TPP development.

### Nguồn gốc: 2. Methods - đoạn 0011

We used our previous TPPs for preterm birth and pre-eclampsia as templates (25,26), which were compiled and published using WHO standard operating procedures for TPP development (16,27). These methods included a literature review, interviews with key stakeholders, an international survey and an online public consultation, in an effort to obtain diverse stakeholder perspectives on obstetric ultrasound devices.

### Nguồn gốc: 2.1 Initial drafting phase - đoạn 0012

WHO, the United States Food and Drug Administration (USFDA), and PATH have published templates for developing TPPs related to obstetric and antenatal care (16–18,27–29). We synthesized data from these existing product templates and generated a new template with 22 key TPP variables for ultrasound use in antenatal care; data from a scoping review of portable ultrasound devices (16); an extensive literature review; and stakeholder consultations with experts and service providers. From this synthesis, we prepared an initial intended use-case scenario and a zero draft of the TPP for ultrasound in antenatal care. Our zero draft TPP included minimum and optimal targets for each variable, rationale and supporting evidence. A TPP development group was established, which included experts involved with the WHO recommendation on imaging ultrasound before 24 weeks of pregnancy.

### Nguồn gốc: 2.1 Initial drafting phase - đoạn 0013

The TPP development group facilitated the overall process, coordinating consultations and synthesizing feedback from a broad range of expert stakeholders external to the group. While the development group did not make independent decisions, it played a key role in ensuring that final decisions were informed by stakeholder consensus and aligned with existing WHO recommendations and guidance. The group worked iteratively to refine the TPP based on this feedback, maintaining consistency with global health priorities and technical feasibility.

### Nguồn gốc: 2.2 Stakeholder identification - đoạn 0014

We identified 10 key stakeholder groups: obstetricians, midwives and nurses, academics or researchers, sonographers or radiologists, antenatal care programme managers, ultrasound manufacturers, representatives of consumer advocacy groups, guideline developers (from relevant professional bodies and organizations), procurement experts, and representatives of international health organizations. Diverse representation from low-, middle- and high-income countries, as well as gender balance, was sought throughout. Specific stakeholder mapping strategies were undertaken to systematically identify relevant stakeholders from each group for participation in our interviews and survey, followed by snowball identification of additional stakeholders (Annex 1).

### Nguồn gốc: 2.2 Stakeholder identification - đoạn 0015

From these mapping results, we contacted 81 stakeholders via email. Our goal was to seek their input on the draft use-case scenario and minimum and optimal targets for each variable in our proposed TPP. Overall, 21 stakeholders participated: Overall, 62% (13 participants) were from high-income countries, 29% (six participants) from lower–middle-income countries, and 9% (two participants) from low-income countries.

Discussions were virtual and conducted in English and each lasted approximately 60 minutes. During each discussion, we reviewed each of the 22 TPP variables sequentially, pausing to emphasize variables within the scope of the participant’s area of expertise. We explicitly sought each participant’s expert opinion on the applicability of such devices for antenatal care across different geographical and wealth strata.

### Nguồn gốc: 2.3 Online survey - đoạn 0016

We conducted an online survey in conjunction with stakeholder interviews to assess consensus on the zero draft TPP. Our main objective was to qualitatively evaluate stakeholders’ agreement with the drafted use-case scenarios and target specifications for each product feature. The survey was pre-tested with three individuals to improve its clarity and functionality. Using the Qualtrics platform (Qualtrics, United States), participants completed a survey where they rated their agreement with the proposed minimum and optimal product feature targets using a Likert scale (30). We aimed for a minimum of 100 responses per feature to determine consensus, defining agreement as 75% or more of participants selecting “agree” or “strongly agree”. Survey dissemination was through email in English, French and Spanish, ensuring informed consent before participation.

### Nguồn gốc: 2.3 Online survey - đoạn 0017

To achieve a broad representation, the survey was active for 10 weeks, with reminders sent after two, four and six weeks.

The survey yielded 261 responses from all WHO regions, of which 18 respondents reported that they had a commercial or financial interest in the development of ultrasound devices. The data were analysed with both the inclusion and exclusion of participants that declared a conflict of interest, which did not alter the consensus findings for any of the 22 variables. Furthermore, the draft TPP was made available online for public consultation on the WHO website. The public consultation period lasted nearly six weeks (concurrent with the online survey). Links to the profile on the WHO website were disseminated via social media platforms to public stakeholders. Overall, consensus was achieved for most product features (>75%), with notable exceptions in pricing. Comments on pricing reflected concerns about affordability and perceived quality.

### Nguồn gốc: 2.3 Online survey - đoạn 0018

The warranty and imaging quality features reached consensus, despite some dissenting opinions on the duration of warranty and the inclusion of advanced imaging technologies. Stakeholder feedback led to modifications in the draft, including changes to pricing language to reflect market competitiveness and affordability in LMICs. The final document incorporated revisions for gender-inclusive language and local language requirements for training and software, adjustments to warranty terms, and the removal of 3D and 4D imaging from imaging quality specifications. The final TPP is available in an online repository (https://www.who.int/tools/target-product-profile-database).

### Nguồn gốc: 3. Summary: intervention use case and - đoạn 0019

target users An affordable ultrasound device will be used for routine clinical evaluation of women in antenatal care settings at least once before 24 weeks of gestation (6). This ultrasound device will be used by appropriately skilled health personnel to estimate GA, through reliable and accurate measurement of fetal biometric parameters such as crown–rump length, biparietal diameter, head circumference, abdominal circumference and femur length. When used by a trained sonographer, the imaging quality should allow for the detection of major fetal anomalies and multiple pregnancies, and assessment of the placenta and umbilical cord/s. When ultrasound devices are available in settings where women give birth, they can also be used in the intrapartum or postpartum period, if deemed appropriate.

### Nguồn gốc: 3.1 Cadre and setting - đoạn 0020

The users are health workers who have undergone the requisite training in using ultrasound safely and correctly. The cadre may vary depending on the setting, but typically includes radiologists, sonologists, ultrasonographers or specialist obstetricians. Increasingly, midwives, nurses, non-physician clinicians or obstetric residents have been trained to use antenatal ultrasound. It should be used in health facility settings where antenatal care is provided.

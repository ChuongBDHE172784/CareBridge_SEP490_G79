Internal evidence seed based on WHO child hospital care guidance. General danger
signs include inability to drink or breastfeed, repeated vomiting, convulsions,
lethargy or unconsciousness, breathing difficulty, and signs consistent with
severe dehydration. These signs support urgent medical assessment and do not
establish a diagnosis.

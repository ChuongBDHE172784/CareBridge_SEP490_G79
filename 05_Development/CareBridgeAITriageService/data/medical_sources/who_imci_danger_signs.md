Internal evidence seed based on WHO IMCI official materials. General danger
signs in children include convulsions, lethargy or unconsciousness, inability to
drink or breastfeed, and severe respiratory or dehydration signs. This evidence
supports urgent risk routing only and is not a diagnosis.

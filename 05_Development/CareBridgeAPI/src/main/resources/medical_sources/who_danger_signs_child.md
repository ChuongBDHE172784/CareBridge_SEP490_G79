title: General danger signs in sick children

topic: danger_signs
ageRange: 0-60 months
---
Danger signs in a sick child include inability to drink or breastfeed, vomiting
everything, convulsions, lethargy or unconsciousness, and breathing difficulty.
When these signs are present, the child needs urgent medical attention rather
than home observation.
